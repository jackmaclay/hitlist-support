# jackmaclay.github.io/hitlist-support

Support and privacy page for [Hitlist](https://apps.apple.com), a small iOS
party game. Started life as a single-app repo, but the plan is for this to
grow into a general landing spot for small/throwaway apps (one page or
subpath per app), rather than creating a new public repo every time an app
needs an App Store support URL.

Why a separate repo at all: the app's own source lives in a private repo,
and GitHub Pages only works on private repos on a paid plan. Keeping the
public-facing page here, decoupled from the app source, means the app repo
can stay private on the free plan.

Not for Wildpack or anything with its own dedicated domain, those are
structured products and route elsewhere.
