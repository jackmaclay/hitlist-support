Support & Privacy

Support

Having a problem with the app, or got a suggestion? Email hello@wildpackapp.com and I'll get back to you.

Privacy

Hitlist doesn't collect any data. There's no analytics, no tracking, no account, and no network connection of any kind.

Everything you enter (player names, weapons, locations, kill history) is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your phone, and I never see it.

If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.
